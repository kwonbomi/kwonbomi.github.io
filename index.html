I work on structured and controllable generative modeling, with a focus on diffusion models that generate structured outputs such as scene graphs and human/robot poses from language.

I am a graduate research assistant in the Data Intelligence Laboratory at Sookmyung Women’s University. My current research explores how generative models can better represent structure, follow language conditions, and satisfy task-specific constraints.

Text-Conditioned Scene Graph Sequence Generation via Discrete Diffusion

A discrete diffusion framework for generating time-ordered scene graph sequences directly from natural-language descriptions of human–object interactions. I led the project end-to-end as first author, from problem formulation and model design to experiments and manuscript preparation.

Current Research2026

Text-Conditioned Humanoid Robot Pose Generation with Physical Constraints

A text-conditioned diffusion model for generating physically valid static poses for the Unitree G1. The work retargets human motion data in MuJoCo and incorporates joint-range and self-collision constraints into generation.

DiffusionPhysical ConstraintsMuJoCoUnitree G1
TKIPS 20262025–2026

Text-driven 2D Human Pose Generation via Diffusion in Directional Angle Space

An angle-based representation for text-driven human pose generation that models skeletal directions instead of directly generating joint coordinates, using diffusion and forward kinematics for reconstruction.
